Hollovar Labs

Small Android applications that do one thing well, and keep to themselves.

Hollovar Labs is an independent developer of Android software. The applications here are built on a simple principle: an application should do its job on your device, and should not need to know anything about you to do it.

Applications

TiltFlow

Hands-free scrolling for Android. Tilt your phone gently to one side and the page scrolls for you, so you can read one-handed without swiping. It works only in the applications you choose.

No accounts, no advertising, no network access.

Privacy

Every Hollovar Labs application has its own privacy policy, and each one says the same thing in different words: nothing is collected, and nothing leaves your device.
